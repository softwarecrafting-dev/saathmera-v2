  2. They tap Good or Not GoodThen answer whether they need any help.

Talk to us
Can my family see my information without asking me?

No. A family member sees nothing until you approve their link request, and you can remove their access at any time from the app.

What happens if my parent misses a check-in?

Nothing happens to their account. The missed day shows up in the family's check-in history, so you can call and make sure they are alright.

Can SaathMera send an ambulance?

No. The Emergency button places a normal phone call to the family member or contact you have chosen. For an ambulance, dial 108.

Does my plan renew automatically?

No. Every payment is a one-time payment. We send a reminder before your plan ends, and you renew only if you want to.
