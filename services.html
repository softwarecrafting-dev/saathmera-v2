Care that comes to your door

Book a visit for yourself or for a parent. Our team arranges the doctor, nurse or laboratory, confirms a time with you, and keeps you updated in the app.

See the services What happens after you book

Book one when you need it, or get it with a plan

Each service can be booked and paid for on its own. Some plans include a number of visits or calls, and those are used first.

₹699per visit

Doctor home visit

A qualified doctor visits your home for a check-up, a new symptom or a follow-up, so your parent does not have to travel or wait at a clinic.

  • Choose a preferred date and time
  • Add a note about what the doctor should know
  • Included in the Monthly Visit, 3 month, 6 month and 12 month plans

Medicines and tests advised by the doctor are paid for separately.

₹399per visit

Nurse home visit

Trained nursing staff for the routine care that is hard to manage alone: injections, wound dressing, and checking blood pressure, sugar and other vitals.

  • Ideal for regular or recurring care
  • Tell us what care is needed when you book
  • Included in the Monthly Care plan

Consumables used during the visit are charged separately.

₹399per call

Doctor call on WhatsApp

A video consultation with a doctor at a time that suits you. The call itself happens on WhatsApp, which most families already use every day.

  • Book the slot in SaathMera, talk on WhatsApp
  • Good for quick questions and follow-ups
  • Included in the Monthly Consult, 3 month, 6 month and 12 month plans

The call happens on WhatsApp, not inside the app.

Test charges set by the laboratory

Blood test at home

Choose the test and a time. Our laboratory partner collects the sample at home, so there is no early morning trip to a lab.

  • Booking arranged by the SaathMera team
  • Booking included in the Monthly Care plan
  • Test charges are billed separately by the laboratory

The laboratory sets the price and bills the test charges separately.

Small things that add up to peace of mind

Beyond the visits, a few everyday features keep the parent and the family close.

Daily check-in

Each morning the app asks how your parent is feeling. One tap answers it, and the family is alerted if they are not well or need help.

Daily Health and Wellness Update

A short, practical tip every day on water, food, movement, medicines and staying well. Included in every plan, from ₹99.

Emergency call button

A button on the home screen that places a normal phone call to the family member or contact you chose. No searching through contacts.

Family access

Link a son, daughter or caregiver. They can see check-ins, book visits and pay, only with the parent's approval.

Timely notifications

Updates when a visit is confirmed or changed, a reminder before a plan ends, and the check-in reminder each morning.

Secure payments

Pay with UPI through Razorpay. Every payment is one time: there is no auto-debit and nothing renews without you.

A real person arranges every visit

A booking is a request. Our team speaks to the doctor, nurse or laboratory and confirms a time that works, then keeps you posted at every step.

  1. You send a requestChoose the service, a preferred date and time, and add any notes.
  2. We arrange itThe SaathMera team finds the right provider and assigns them to your visit.
  3. You get an updateYour visit is confirmed, or moved to a time that works if the first choice is not available.
  4. The visit happensOnce complete, it appears in your history and you can rate how it went.

If a visit cannot go ahead

  • If we cannot arrange a visit, you are told why and any payment is refunded.
  • Cancel before the visit and your payment is refunded, or the visit returns to your plan.
  • Refunds go back through Razorpay and usually arrive in 3 to 5 working days.

SaathMera is not an emergency service. It does not dispatch or track ambulances and does not use your location. In a medical emergency, dial 108.
