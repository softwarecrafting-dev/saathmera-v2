One app. The parent stays in charge.

Parents and family members use the same SaathMera app. When you sign up you choose your role, and the app shows you what you need, nothing more.

For parents For families

Set up once, then one tap a day

Large text, big buttons and plain words throughout. Most days, the only thing to do is answer the check-in.

  1. Install and choose "I am the parent"Sign up with your mobile number and confirm it with a one-time code.
  2. Add your detailsYour address for home visits, and the person the Emergency button should call.
  3. Share your link code with familyRead it out or send it. You approve every request before anyone can see anything.
  4. Answer the morning check-inGood or Not Good. If you need help, say what kind and send a short message.
  5. Book care when you need itDoctor, nurse, blood test or a WhatsApp call, at a time that suits you.

Stay close, even from another city

You see what your parent has agreed to share, and you can act on it straight away.

  1. Install and choose "I am family"Sign up with your own mobile number. Your account is separate from your parent's.
  2. Enter your parent's link codeYour request waits until your parent approves it in their app.
  3. Follow their check-insSee each day's answer, any missed days, and get an alert when they need you.
  4. Book and pay on their behalfArrange a visit to their address and follow it until it is done.
  5. Look after their planSee what is left, and renew or upgrade when the time comes.

Nobody sees anything without the parent's yes

Family access is built around consent. The parent decides who is linked and what they can do, and can change their mind at any time.

A code, then an approval

The family member enters the parent's link code, and the parent approves or declines. Link codes expire and can be replaced with a new one.

Separate permissions

Seeing check-ins, booking visits and paying are separate permissions. The parent can allow one without the others.

Revoke at any time

From the Family access screen the parent can remove a family member. Their access ends straight away.

From request to visit

  1. Choose a service

    Doctor home visit, nurse home visit, blood test at home or a doctor call on WhatsApp.

  2. Pick a time

    Choose a preferred date and time, and add a note for the doctor or nurse.

  3. Pay or use your plan

    Pay with UPI, or use a visit included in your plan if you have one left.

  4. We confirm it

    Our team arranges the provider and you get a notification as soon as it is confirmed.

Bring SaathMera home

Free to download on Android. Choose a plan, or pay per visit, whenever you need care.
