How plans and payments work

The fine print, in plain words.

Visits come back if a booking falls through

If an included visit or consultation is cancelled or cannot be arranged before it happens, it goes back into your plan.

Unused visits end with the plan

Included visits and consultations do not carry forward once the plan expires. The app shows how many you have left.

One-time payments with UPI

Payments are handled by Razorpay. There is no auto-debit and no automatic renewal. We send a reminder before your plan ends.

Family can pay

A linked family member with permission to pay can buy or renew a parent's plan and pay for their visits.

Prices are in Indian rupees. Refunds go back to the original payment method and usually arrive within 3 to 5 working days. Prices shown here may change; the app always shows the current price before you pay.
